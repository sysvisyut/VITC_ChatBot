# VIT Men's Hostel Code of Conduct
**Document Code:** VITCCMH-2020  
**Institution:** Vellore Institute of Technology (Deemed to be University under section 3 of UGC Act, 1956)  
**Target:** VIT Men's Hostel

## Disciplinary Rules and Action Matrix

| Sl. No. | Violation | Action to be Taken* |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | Avoiding giving attendance in the Hostel being present in the night | **1st Time Defaulters:** Warning<br>**2nd Time Defaulters:** Outing Cancellation for 15 days<br>**On Repetition:** Outing Cancellation for one semester |
| **02** | **a)** Entering Campus Gate / Hostel Block Gate Without ID Card<br>**b)** Misuse of Student ID Card | **a)** Penalty of Rs.100/-<br>**b) 1st Time:** Penalty of Rs.100/-<br>**On Repetition:** Penalty of Rs.100/- + No priority in Hostel Room Counselling. |
| **03** | Giving false Information During Enquiry for misconduct | Outing Cancellation for three months |
| **04** | Late coming | **1st Time:** Penalty of Rs. 100/-<br>**2nd Time:** Penalty of Rs. 200/-<br>**3rd Time:** Penalty of Rs. 500/-<br>**On Repetition:** No priority in Hostel room counselling |
| **05** | Possession of Electrical appliances inside Hostel room | Penalty of Rs.500/- + Confiscation of electrical appliances which will be returned to the Student after the completion of Degree.<br>**On Repetition:** No Priority in hostel room allotment |
| **06** | Smoking | **1st Time:** Penalty of Rs. 3000 + Community Service (Participate in Anti-smoking Campaign + Social Services*) + Counselling<br>**On Repetition:** Penalty of Rs.5000/- + No Priority in Hostel room allotment |
| **07** | Absent without obtaining leave (AWOL) | **1st Time:** Penalty of Rs. 3000/-<br>**On Repetition:** Penalty of Rs.5000/- + No Priority in Hostel room allotment |
| **08** | Celebration of Birthday/any kind of party in the hostel by disturbing the peaceful environment during silence hours (9:00pm to 6:00am) | **1st Time:** Penalty of Rs. 3000/- to each participant<br>**2nd Time:** Penalty of Rs.5000/- |
| **09** | Bringing/Allowing any Day Scholar/ Day Boarder/ Non-VITian Inside the hostel | Penalty of Rs. 5000/- |
| **10** | Damaging the University's Property | Penalty of Rs. 5000/- + Payment of three times the cost of damage (Social Services*). |
| **11** | Fighting/Slander/Quarrelling Resulting in:<br>**a)** Minor Injury<br>**b)** Major Injury | **a)** Penalty of Rs.5000/- + No Priority in Hostel Room Counselling (Social Services*).<br>**b)** Expulsion from the university (Social Services*). |
| **12** | Bringing any kind of Motor Vehicles inside the Hostel area | Penalty of Rs. 5000/- and keeping the Vehicle in Safe Custody (Social Services*). |
| **13** | **Alcohol:**<br>**a)** Coming in Drunken State / Inducing others to consume alcohol<br>**b)** Bringing Liquor / Possession of liquor / Drinking inside the Hostel / Possession of even empty liquor bottle | **a) 1st Time:** Suspension for One Semester<br>**2nd Time:** Suspension for One Year<br>**b) 1st Time:** Suspension for One Year<br>**2nd Time:** Suspension for Two Years |
| **14** | Stealing/Theft | One year suspension from the University |
| **15** | Any kind of Cyber Crime / Defaming VIT University's name in any Manner | Expulsion from the University |
| **16** | Forgery/Multilevel Marketing/Gambling | Expulsion from the University |
| **17** | Ragging/Harassment of fellow students in any manner | Expulsion from the University |
| **18** | Drugs/Substance abuse | Expulsion from the University |

---

## Additional Guidelines and Notes

*\* In addition to the penalties, students should also be involved in social activities (serving in the orphanages, old age homes), student's welfare activities and NSS based on the nature of violation.*

### Key Notes:
1. All violations will be reported to the parents.
2. A record of Student's Hostel rules violations will be maintained in the students' personal file.
3. Students involved in major violations/ repeated violations will lose privileges such as scholarship and placement services.
4. All penalties need to be paid as donation to charity organisations (as specified in the order).
